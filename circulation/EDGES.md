
The controllers under `circulation/` are not seventh organs. They own no Card state, capability state, implementation occupancy, evidence profile, authority, or promotion state.

A known invalid coupling exists outside this repository: Platform references Registry through a hardcoded preview URL. It is not admitted because the address is unstable and the canonical Registry identity is absent. The source repository was not supplied here, so removal is not claimed.

## Adding a live edge

An admitted edge declares:

```text
producer and consumer
canonical identities
what crosses
versioned contract
owner of durable state
failure and replay semantics
authority and evidence requirements
admission artifact
```

If no contract governs the boundary, the contract is written before the edge. If an adapter begins owning institutional meaning, the edge has become an organ and must be rejected.
