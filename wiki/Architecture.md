# Architecture

The Super Bundle combines **Process** and **Organism**.

- **Process** owns authority, admission, run consequence, and execution custody.
- **Organism** owns Cards, Heartime, roster, and reconciliation.
- **Registry** (separate repository) remains source of Identity, Office/Occupancy, Brand, Store, and artifact lineage.
