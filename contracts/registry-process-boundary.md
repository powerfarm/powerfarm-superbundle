Registry and Process are both durable PowerFarm components, but they answer different questions.

## Registry owns

- `person`, `office`, `app` identities;
- Office/Occupancy continuity;
- identity keys and external identity links;
- Brand and Manifest;
- Store workspaces and local editor ACLs;
- Gadget drafts, immutable revisions, installations and exact artifact lineage.

## Process owns

- responsibility/authority grants and revocations;
- run authority and admission;
- Process runs and causal consequences;
- approvals/commits where they are institutional consequences;
- execution custody through versioned Settings (`continuum-adk`, `continuum-ai-sdk`);
- evidence/proofs required to show why a consequence was admissible.

## Law

A Registry workspace role is never a PowerFarm authority grant.
A Registry capability resolution is never an execution authorization.
An engine callback or AI SDK tool call never creates authority.
Process references exact Registry identity/artifact revisions and records the authority actually spent.
