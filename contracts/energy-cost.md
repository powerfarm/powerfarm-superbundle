`pf.contract.energy-cost.v1` makes resource use part of Card circulation.

Process authorizes capacity. Heartime decides whether the Card still has enough authorized energy to circulate and admits metering observations into the Card. Platform reports execution measurements with durable evidence. Homeostasis derives pressure from the resulting state.

Energy is deliberately a vector. Tokens, tool calls, time and human attention do not become a fictional common unit. Monetary cost is represented as integer micro-units under an explicit currency.

A Heartime beat is itself energy: every successful emission consumes one `beats` unit. Recovery reissues therefore consume additional beat capacity while preserving the same institutional attempt and run identity.

Platform observations are content-addressed, tied to the exact Card/attempt/beat, and evidence-backed. Replay of the same observation is a no-op. If real consumption exceeds authorization, PowerFarm records the truth and blocks subsequent executable circulation rather than hiding the overdraw.

ExecutionSlice v3 carries the remaining resource budget to every engine Setting. That budget is execution context, never run identity.

Homeostasis may project `NORMAL`, `WATCH`, `CRITICAL`, or `EXHAUSTED` resource pressure plus `circulatory_debt`, which means energy/cost has been spent without durable work evidence yet returning to the Card.
