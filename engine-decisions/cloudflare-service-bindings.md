**Status:** First Seam settings implemented and locally contract-tested; not deployed

## Role

Private Worker-to-Worker invocation for:

- Heartime → attention reconciler;
- attention reconciler → organ-owned port entrypoints.

## Mechanism supplied

- private RPC without public preview URLs;
- provider-local routing;
- typed method-shaped invocation at the Worker boundary.

## PowerFarm meaning retained above it

- institutional references;
- port contract versions;
- Card semantics;
- Office/Occupancy;
- authority;
- run/effect meaning;
- Evidence.

A Service Binding name is not institutional identity.

## Security boundary

Authentication/Access context is not assumed to propagate implicitly. PowerFarm identity and authority remain explicit in the organ contracts and are resolved by the owning organs.

## What survives removal

The v1 contracts, portable controller, Heartime state, and all institutional references. Another private RPC mechanism can occupy this setting.

## Conformance

- public HTTP handlers return 404;
- contract versions are checked;
- Card payload cannot enter through the Heartime wake;
- provider-local names never replace `pf.*` references.
