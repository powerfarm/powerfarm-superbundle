PowerFarm is enterprise infrastructure for one individual, made possible by
intelligences that compile complexity into code and grammar.

That sentence is load-bearing in both directions. The intelligences make the
infrastructure operable by one person. The infrastructure makes the
intelligences accountable.

---

## 1. The intelligence operates the automation. It is not the automation.

A model does not become the mechanism by running inside one. It sits at a
surface and proposes acts, the same surface a person sits at.

This is why an institutional act exists once and is rendered twice: as pixels
for a person, as a tool definition for an operator. Neither rendering owns the
meaning. Neither rendering grants power the other lacks.

**Negative control.** An institutional act reachable by an agent and not by a
person, or by a person and not by an agent, breaks this claim. So does any
path that mutates institutional state without passing an Action Contract.
Administrative operations — disaster recovery, cryptographic bootstrap,
physical key enrollment, break-glass — are legitimately human-only, and are
excepted only while they remain a named and audited category.

## 2. The infrastructure contains the intelligence.

An engine does not acquire the right to act by deciding to call a tool. It
calls, and admission answers. Institutional identity is never derived from
engine-local session, invocation or model state.

**Negative control.** An external effect produced without a sealed
ExecutionSlice, or an engine whose local memory becomes authoritative over
PowerFarm MEMORY, breaks this claim.

## 3. Capability is bounded by where an intelligence sits, and it sits temporarily.

An Office is not a person and not a department. It is a durable envelope:
permissions, tools, and the definition of what occupies it — model, engine,
prompt, as content-addressed definitions rather than names.

Occupancy is the temporary part. The chair outlives whoever sits in it, and
replacing the occupant invalidates the previous one immediately.

**Negative control.** Authority that survives the occupancy that carried it,
or an occupant that widens its own envelope, breaks this claim.

## 4. PowerFarm does not shrink enterprise infrastructure.

It does not scale down. It does not simplify Authority, custody, lineage or
consequence because only one person is present. It keeps the whole apparatus
and relies on intelligences to absorb its operation organically.

The usual pitch inverts this — make the enterprise thing small enough for one
person to hold. PowerFarm holds that the apparatus is what makes one person's
work defensible over years, and that removing it to fit a single operator
removes the reason to have built it.

**Negative control.** A capability removed because a single operator cannot
carry it by hand breaks this claim. The correct response to that pressure is
an office that carries it, not a smaller institution.

## 5. The individual is preserved because his duties are elsewhere.

The Director's attention is the scarcest thing in the system. It is spent on
decisions that require legitimacy, and on nothing else.

An interruption the Director cannot act on is not an escalation. It is a
notification, and notifications to the Director are a defect in the escalation
rule rather than an acceptable cost. Fixing a dead service is an operator act.
Authorizing what is irreversible, or what changes what the institution means,
is a Director act — even when the Director could not perform the repair
himself.

**Negative control.** An escalation whose recorded outcome is that it should
not have escalated, occurring repeatedly without the rule changing, breaks
this claim.

## 6. The human also works through a limited grammar.

The person does not receive a privileged write because he is a person. He
proposes acts through the same contracts, and the institution admits or refuses
them on the same terms.

The exception is exactly one, and it is structural rather than a loophole: the
Director's decision mints authority instead of consuming it. What grants that
is the occupancy of the Director office, never the interface the occupant
happens to be sitting at.

**Negative control.** A human-only path that writes institutional state
outside an Action Contract, or an interface that grants power by virtue of
being an interface, breaks this claim.

## 7. Automation Max is the surface, for both kinds of intelligence.

Some intelligences are ephemeral. They wake for one act, do it, and are gone;
their reasoning is not preserved and should not be relied upon.

Some are durable. Offices, the ledger, Heartime, the Director. They persist,
carry obligation, and are answerable later.

Automation Max is the commitment that both receive the best available surface,
and the same one. The ephemeral gets an act it cannot misunderstand: fully
specified, nothing implied by what is on screen. The durable gets an act it
can still explain in two years.

Where the two pull apart — bulk operations are the first place they do — the
resolution is that the batch is itself an act with its own admission, not a
convenience laid over acts that were designed one at a time.

**Negative control.** A surface serving one kind of intelligence at the
expense of the other breaks this claim. So does an act whose meaning depends
on what was visible when it was proposed.
